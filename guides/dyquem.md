# Château d'Yquem Research Notes

## Bottle format

Half bottles (375 ml) are particularly useful for Sauternes because the wine is rich, often consumed in smaller pours, and can be difficult to finish from a 750 ml bottle in one sitting.

For mature vintages, smaller formats can also make a high-cost bottle easier to open for a normal dinner rather than reserving it for a large group.

## Mature-vintage buying

For older d'Yquem, headline vintage reputation is only one input. Also evaluate provenance, fill level, capsule and cork condition, color relative to age, storage history, merchant reputation, bottle size, and auction-vs.-retail return protections.

## Vintage research

When comparing vintages, separate three questions:

1. How strong was the vintage at release?
2. How is the wine expected to evolve at its current age?
3. Is the specific bottle's provenance strong enough to realize that potential?

For very old bottles, question 3 can matter more than small differences in critic scores.
