# Occasion Pairing

Prestige Champagne can be chosen by the role the bottle should play in the event rather than by prestige alone.

## Cristal

Often works well for a polished, ceremonial setting where elegance, visual presentation, and a clean celebratory identity matter.

## Dom Pérignon

An iconic milestone bottle with broad recognition. Vintage selection and maturity can make it either a straightforward celebratory Champagne or a more serious wine-centered experience.

## Krug

Krug's broader, richer style and Grande Cuvée concept make it particularly natural when food and the dinner itself are central to the occasion.

## Late releases

Extended-maturation releases such as Dom Pérignon P2 or comparable late-release prestige Champagne make the most sense when the wine itself is intended to be a major part of the experience, rather than simply a toast.

## General rule

Match the bottle to the event's function: toast/ceremony, dinner pairing, wine-focused tasting, milestone gift, or mature-wine comparison.
